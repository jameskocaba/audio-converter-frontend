    Frequently Asked Questions

    1. How do I back up my SoundCloud tracks to MP3?

    Simply copy the shareable URL from SoundCloud, paste it into the search box on mp3aud.io, and click the "Convert to MP3" button. Our system will automatically process the link and generate a high-quality MP3 backup for you to save.

    2. Why should I create a backup of my SoundCloud playlist?

    Files on SoundCloud are often removed due to license changes, artist account deletions, or copyright updates. Creating a local file backup ensures that you have permanent access to your favorite media, even if the original link goes offline.

    3. What audio quality are the MP3 backups?

    We prioritize audio fidelity. Our tool attempts to capture the highest available bitrate provided by the shareable link. This ensures your backup sounds as close to the original stream as possible, typically 128kbps depending on the source file.

    4. Is mp3aud.io publicly accessible?

    Yes. Currently, our SoundCloud backup tool is 100% publicaly accessible. There is no software to install and no registration required. We provide a clean, fast interface for users to archive media instantly.

    5. How many files can I convert at once?

    You can convert up to 50 files per playlist. Files are processed one at a time sequentially to ensure maximum reliability and prevent server overload. You'll see real-time progress as each track completes.

    6. Do I need to keep the browser tab open?

    Yes, you need to keep the browser tab open during the conversion process. Closing the tab will stop the download progress.

    7. How many simulteanous conversions are allowed?

    Currently, we support only one playlist conversion at a time for an ehanced user experience. Users will be placed in queue when there is an ongoing active conversion.
