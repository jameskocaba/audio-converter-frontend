    Frequently Asked Questions

    1. How do I back up my media to MP3?

    Simply copy the shareable URL, paste it into the search box, set any optional start/end times, and click "Convert to MP3". Our system will process the link and generate a ZIP file containing your high-quality MP3s.

    2. What does the "Generate AI" checkbox do?

    When checked, our backend uses advanced AI (OpenAI Whisper and GPT-4o) to transcribe the audio file. It then intelligently detects if the audio is a "DIY/Instructional Video" or a "Professional Meeting" and generates a formatted HTML document containing step-by-step instructions, safety warnings, or meeting minutes and action items. These text files are included in your final ZIP download.

    3. How do the Start and End time fields work?

    If you only want a specific segment of an audio file, you can input a Start Time (e.g., 01:20) and an End Time (e.g., 05:45). Our processor will clip the audio to those exact timestamps before converting and transcribing it.

    4. What audio quality are the MP3 backups?

    We prioritize audio fidelity. Our tool attempts to capture the highest available bitrate provided by the shareable link (typically 128kbps) preserving the metadata and frequency response of the original stream.

    5. How many files can I convert at once?

    You can convert up to 50 files per playlist URL. Files are processed sequentially to ensure maximum reliability. You'll see real-time progress as each track downloads, extracts, and transcribes.

    6. Do I need to keep the browser tab open?

    If you enter your email address before converting, you do not need to keep the tab open. We will email you a secure download link once your ZIP file is ready. Note: This link automatically expires after 1 hour.

    7. How long will my conversion take?

    Because processing high-quality audio and running AI transcription is resource-intensive, conversions are handled in a queue. If the server is busy, you will be given a queue position and an estimated wait time (averaging about 45 seconds per track).

    Privacy Policy

    We prioritize your privacy. We do not store user data or IP logs, and all generated files are permanently deleted from our servers shortly after processing.

    Data Collection & Retention:

    • Email Addresses: If provided, emails are used strictly by our email provider to send a one-time "Download Ready" notification. They are not stored permanently or sold.
    • Your Files: Converted MP3s, ZIP archives, and generated text files are temporarily stored on our server to allow you to download them. They are automatically and permanently deleted 1 hour after the last update to your session.

    Third-Party AI Processing:

    • If you choose to use the AI Transcription feature, your audio files are temporarily securely transmitted to OpenAI's API to generate transcripts and summaries. OpenAI does not use data submitted via their API to train their models.

    Cookies: We use Google Analytics to understand site usage and improve our service. You can opt out using browser settings or extensions.
